FAQ

QUESTIONS, ANSWERED.

Still unsure? Call us on +91 XXXXX XXXXX or drop by — we are happy to show you around.

Is there a joining fee?

No joining fee on 12-month plans. Monthly and quarterly plans carry a one-time ₹999 enrolment fee.

Can I try the gym before joining?

Yes — book a free trial day or a trial week. You will meet a coach and get a free fitness assessment.

I have never trained before. Is that okay?

Absolutely. Most of our members started as beginners. Your coach builds a programme at your level.

Can I freeze or transfer my membership?

Quarterly and annual plans can be frozen for up to 30 days a year and transferred once with a small admin fee.

What are your opening hours?

Monday to Saturday 5:00 AM – 11:00 PM, Sunday 6:00 AM – 10:00 PM. Group classes follow the weekly timetable.

Do you offer lockers, showers and parking?

Yes. Secure lockers, hot showers, towels on request and free two-wheeler and car parking.
