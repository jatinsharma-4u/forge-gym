Our story

FROM A GARAGE TO A GYM.

Every plate, rack and coach here exists for one reason: to help you get measurably better. Fifteen years on, that is still the only metric we care about.

  1. 2010

    The first rack

    Forge opens in a 2,000 sq ft garage with six racks and a handful of obsessive lifters.

  2. 2014

    A real home

    We move into our current 18,000 sq ft facility with a full strength floor and cardio zone.

  3. 2018

    First champions

    Our members start placing at state and national powerlifting and fitness meets.

  4. 2021

    Coaching academy

    Launch of our in-house coach education programme and group training studio.

  5. 2025

    5,000 members

    A community of five thousand people training, recovering and evolving together.
